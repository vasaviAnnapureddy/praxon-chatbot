# Privacy and data handling

> PLACEHOLDER CONTENT — this is written to reflect India's DPDP Act basics for a
> demo. Have it reviewed before Praxon publishes it as a legal page.

## What this chat assistant collects

When you talk to the assistant on this website, we store:

- The messages you send and the assistant's replies.
- Details you choose to share: what you need built, your budget range, your
  timeline, your name, your company, and your email or phone number.
- A random session identifier so the conversation holds together while you chat.
- Basic technical information: approximate time, and a shortened form of your IP
  address used only to stop abuse and spam.

We do **not** ask for and do not want: passwords, payment card details, bank
details, Aadhaar, PAN, or any government identity number. Please do not type
those into the chat.

## Why we collect it

One reason only: so a human from the Praxon team can understand your enquiry and
reply to it. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to build
profiles of you.

## Consent

Before the assistant asks for your name, email or phone, it tells you what the
information is for. Sharing it is your choice. You can use the entire assistant,
ask anything, and get complete answers without ever giving a contact detail.

If you skip a question, the assistant will not ask it again in that conversation.

## Who can see it

Only the Praxon team, through a password-protected dashboard. The data lives in a
managed PostgreSQL database with encrypted connections. Backups are encrypted and
stored privately.

## How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept while the enquiry is active and for a reasonable period
afterwards for follow-up. Records are deleted on request.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- Tell you what we hold about you.
- Correct anything that is wrong.
- Delete your enquiry and the conversation attached to it.

Email **hello@praxontech.in** with the request. We action deletion requests
promptly.

## Automated processing

The assistant is an AI system, not a person, and it will tell you so if you ask.
It reads the conversation to pick out your requirement, budget and timeline so
the team has context before replying.

It does **not** score you, rank you, or decide whether you are worth contacting.
A person at Praxon reads every enquiry and decides. No decision that affects you
is made automatically.

## Logs

Technical logs are kept for a short period to diagnose faults. Email addresses
and phone numbers are masked in logs.

## Cookies

The chat uses your browser's local storage to remember your session id so your
conversation is not lost if you reload the page. That is it — no advertising or
tracking cookies are set by the assistant.

## Security

- All traffic uses HTTPS.
- API keys stay on the server and are never sent to your browser.
- The team dashboard is password protected with login rate limiting.
- Backups are encrypted and stored in a private location.

## Accuracy

The assistant answers from Praxon's published content. It is instructed not to
invent facts, prices or claims, and to say that it does not know rather than
guess. If something it said turns out to be wrong or unclear, the written scope
and the signed agreement are what count — not the chat.
