# Frequently asked questions

> PLACEHOLDER CONTENT — replace with Praxon's real answers.

**What does Praxon do?**
We build software: web applications, business websites, mobile apps, AI
assistants and chatbots, and cloud setups. We also maintain software after
launch. We are based in Hyderabad, Telangana.

**Where are you located?**
Hyderabad, Telangana, India. We work with clients anywhere in India and remotely
outside it.

**How much will my project cost?**
It depends on scope, so we publish ranges rather than fixed prices. A business
website is typically ₹35,000 to ₹1,20,000; a web application ₹1,50,000 to
₹6,00,000; a mobile app ₹2,00,000 to ₹8,00,000; an AI chatbot ₹75,000 to
₹3,00,000. We confirm an exact number in writing after a short call.

**How long will it take?**
A website is usually 2 to 4 weeks, a web application 6 to 12 weeks, a mobile app
8 to 16 weeks, a chatbot 3 to 6 weeks. Delays almost always come from waiting on
approvals and content, not from development.

**Do you sign an NDA?**
Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before seeing anything confidential, and we can sign it
before the first call if you prefer. We are happy to sign yours.

**Who owns the code?**
You do. Full IP transfers to you on final payment. The repository sits in your
account, and cloud and domain accounts are in your name.

**Will you maintain it after launch?**
Yes. Every project includes 30 days of bug fixing on delivered scope, and monthly
maintenance plans start at around ₹8,000 per month depending on what needs
covering.

**Can you fix or take over software another company built?**
Usually yes, after a paid code review so we know what we are inheriting. If the
honest answer is that rebuilding costs less than repairing, we will tell you
that.

**Do you work with startups?**
Yes. For early-stage products we usually recommend a tight phase 1 that proves
the idea works, rather than building the full vision before a single user has
touched it.

**What if I don't know exactly what I need?**
That is normal and it is what the discovery call is for. Come with the problem,
not the specification. We will write the specification with you.

**Do you offer a trial or a small first project?**
Yes — for new clients who are understandably cautious about a new company, we
suggest a small paid pilot: one module or a two-week scoped piece of work, so you
can see how we operate before committing a full budget.

**How do we communicate during the project?**
A shared WhatsApp or Slack group, a weekly written update every week, a named
technical point of contact, and a clickable working link at the end of every
two-week milestone.

**What are your working hours?**
10:00 to 19:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Critical production issues are covered
outside those hours under a support plan.

**Do you provide hosting?**
We set up hosting in your name and manage it if you want us to. You pay the cloud
provider directly, so you are never locked to us for access to your own servers.

**Do you take equity instead of payment?**
No. We take cash for work. It keeps everyone's expectations clean.

**Can you start immediately?**
Usually within one to two weeks of scope sign-off. If we genuinely cannot start
when you need, we will say so rather than take the project and sit on it.

**Do you work on fixed price or hourly?**
Fixed price against a written scope for defined projects. Hourly (₹1,200 to
₹2,500 per hour) for small ad-hoc changes and ongoing advisory.

**Is my data safe with you?**
We only collect what is needed, we sign NDAs, and we do not share client
information with anyone outside the project team. See our policies page for
details.

**Are you hiring?**
See the careers page. Write to careers@praxontech.in with your resume and one
short paragraph on what you have actually built.

**How do I start?**
Message us on WhatsApp, book a 30-minute discovery call, or leave your email here
and the team will reply within one working day.
