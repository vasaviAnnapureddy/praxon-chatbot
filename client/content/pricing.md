**These are typical ranges, not quotes.** The real number depends on scope. We
confirm it in writing after a short call, and that written number is what you pay
for that scope.

## What moves the price up

- Number of distinct screens and user roles.
- Payment gateways, third-party integrations, government APIs.
- Custom design versus adapting a well-chosen template.
- Real-time features (live chat, tracking, notifications).
- Migrating data out of an existing system.
- Compliance requirements and audits.
- A compressed deadline that needs more people in parallel.

## What keeps the price down

- A clear, written list of what you need before we start.
- Content and images ready on time.
- Phase 1 that solves the actual problem, with the wish-list in phase 2.
- Reusing proven components instead of building everything bespoke.
- Fast feedback — approvals inside two working days.

## What is included in every project

Responsive mobile-first build, basic SEO setup for websites, HTTPS, a staging
link for review, deployment to your accounts, code repository handover,
documentation, a walkthrough session, and 30 days of bug fixing on delivered
scope.

## What is charged separately

Domain and hosting fees (paid by you, directly, in your name), paid third-party
licences or APIs, stock photography, content writing, and new features requested
after scope is signed.

## Payment schedule

Milestone based. Typically 30% to begin, milestone payments through the build,
balance on launch. Invoices are raised against delivered and accepted milestones.

## Working with a smaller budget

If your budget is below our typical range, tell us anyway — it is a starting
point, not a rejection. We will be straight with you about what a full custom
build costs, and then work out what we can do well within your number.

Usually that means a focused first phase: only the pages or features you need
to get started, built properly, with a clear path to add the rest later as your
business grows. Many good products start exactly this way. A short call is the
quickest way to find the right first step.
