We were formed in 2026 by engineers who wanted to work differently: small senior

## How we work with clients

**You work with the engineers, not with account managers.** The person who
understands your project is the person writing the code. Nothing is lost in
translation, and decisions are made in the same conversation they are raised.

**We commit to a written scope and a date.** Before anything is built you receive
a document setting out exactly what will be delivered, what is excluded, and what
it costs. We hold ourselves to that document.

**You see working software every two weeks.** Not a status report — a link you
can open and use. Progress is something you verify, not something you take on
trust.

**Everything is yours.** The code, the repositories, the servers, the domain and
the third-party accounts are in your name from the first day and remain yours
permanently. We are engaged to build your software, not to hold it.

## Where we work

Our base is Hyderabad, Telangana. We work with clients throughout India and,
remotely, with clients outside it. For Hyderabad clients we are happy to meet in
person whenever it is useful; for everyone else, a scheduled call at a time that
suits you.

## Questions clients ask before starting

**"Praxon is a young company. What assurance do I have?"**

Three things, all of them contractual rather than verbal.

Your intellectual property transfers to you on final payment, and the repository
sits in your account from day one. Your cloud, domain and payment accounts are
created in your name with you as the owner, so your software never depends on our
continued involvement. And we work to a written scope with fixed milestones, so
you are paying against delivered work rather than against a promise.

For clients who would prefer to see how we work before committing to a full
engagement, we are glad to begin with a scoped pilot — a single module or a
two-week piece of work. You judge the code, the communication and the delivery
dates for yourself, and then decide.

**"Can I see examples of your work?"**

On a call we will walk you through our technical approach in detail: how we scope
a project, the standards we hold our code to, the architecture we would propose
for your requirement, and work the team has delivered previously where we are
free to discuss it. Much of our work sits under confidentiality agreements, and
we treat those as seriously for past clients as we will for you.

**"How large is the team?"**

We are a small senior team and we assemble the right people for each project
rather than keeping a bench occupied. Before work begins you will know exactly
who is assigned to your project and what each of them is responsible for.

**"What happens after launch?"**

Every project includes thirty days of support on the delivered scope at no extra
cost. Beyond that we offer monthly maintenance covering security updates,
dependency upgrades, fixes, small enhancements and uptime monitoring. And if you
ever choose to take the work in-house or to another partner, we hand over
completely and document it properly.

## What we hold ourselves to

- We deliver on the date we committed to.
- If something is going to slip, you hear it early, not on the deadline.
- We write code the next engineer can read, because it may not always be us.
- We tell you plainly when something is outside our expertise, and who to speak
  to instead.
