## The six steps

**1. Discovery call (free, 30 minutes)**
We ask what the problem is, who uses it, and what "done" looks like. No slides.
If we are not the right fit, we say so on this call.

**2. Scope and estimate (2 to 4 working days)**
We write down exactly what will be built, what is explicitly out of scope, the
milestones, and a price. Not a range at this stage — a number, tied to that
written scope.

**3. Design and approval**
Wireframes first, then screens. You approve the screens before any production
code is written, because changing a screen is cheap and changing built software
is not.

**4. Build in two-week milestones**
At the end of every milestone you get a working link you can click, not a status
report. Weekly written update: what shipped, what is next, anything at risk.

**5. Testing and launch**
Functional testing, mobile testing on real devices, load sanity checks, security
basics. Then deployment to your accounts, your domain, your servers.

**6. Handover and support**
You receive the code repository, deployment documentation, admin credentials and
a walkthrough session. Then an optional monthly maintenance plan.

## Timelines, realistically

| Work | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Business website (5-8 pages) | 2 to 4 weeks |
| Web application / dashboard | 6 to 12 weeks |
| Mobile app (Android + iOS) | 8 to 16 weeks |
| AI chatbot / automation | 3 to 6 weeks |
| Small changes on existing software | 2 to 10 working days |

These assume feedback comes back within two working days. The single biggest
cause of delay on any project is waiting for approvals and content, not
development.

## Payments

Milestone based, typically 30% to start, then tied to delivered milestones, with
the remainder on launch. GST applies. We invoice against what has been delivered
and accepted.

## Contracts, NDA and ownership

**NDA:** Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before the discovery call if you want, and
always before we see anything confidential. Ask and we will send ours, or we sign
yours.

**Code ownership:** Yours. Full intellectual property in the delivered work
transfers to you on final payment. The repository is created in your GitHub or
handed to your account.

**Accounts:** Cloud, domain, payment gateway and third-party accounts are created
in your name with you as owner. We get access, not ownership.

**Third-party components:** We use open-source libraries under permissive
licences. We list them on handover. If a project needs a paid licence, we tell
you before it is used.

**Source code escrow:** Available on request for larger engagements.

## Communication

- One WhatsApp or Slack group with your team and ours.
- A weekly written update, every week, without being asked.
- A named point of contact who is technical.
- Working hours 10:00 to 19:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Critical production issues
  are handled outside those hours under a support plan.

## After launch

- 30 days of free bug fixing on delivered scope. A bug is something that does not
  work as specified — new requests are new work, quoted separately.
- Optional monthly maintenance from then on.
- If you decide to take the software in-house or to another vendor, we hand over
  cleanly and document it. No hostages.
