## 1. Web application development

Custom web applications and internal dashboards: admin panels, booking systems,
inventory and billing tools, customer portals, CRM-style trackers, reporting
dashboards.

Typical stack: React on the front end, Python (FastAPI/Django) or Node on the
back end, PostgreSQL for data, deployed to AWS, Azure or a managed host.

Good fit when: a spreadsheet or a WhatsApp group is currently doing the job and
it has stopped scaling.

## 2. Business websites

Marketing and company websites: five to fifteen pages, mobile-first, fast on
Indian mobile networks, SEO basics done properly, a CMS so the client's own team
can edit text and images without calling us, contact forms that actually deliver
mail, and analytics.

Good fit when: there is no website, or the current one is slow, unreadable on a
phone, or cannot be edited without a developer.

## 3. Mobile app development

Android and iOS apps from one codebase using React Native or Flutter, native
when the app genuinely needs it. Includes app store submission, push
notifications, offline handling, payments integration (Razorpay, Stripe) and
post-launch updates.

Good fit when: customers need to do something repeatedly, on the move, or
offline.

## 4. AI and chatbot solutions

This is the category this very assistant belongs to.

- Website assistants that answer from a company's real content (RAG), route job
  queries, and pass qualified enquiries to the team.
- WhatsApp and support bots that handle repeat questions and hand off to a human
  cleanly.
- Document intelligence: pulling structured data out of invoices, resumes,
  forms, contracts.
- Internal assistants that search a company's own documents and answer staff
  questions.
- Automation of repetitive back-office steps.

We build these to be grounded in the client's own content, so the assistant does
not invent facts about the business.

## 5. Cloud and DevOps

Cloud setup and migration on AWS and Azure, Docker containerisation, CI/CD
pipelines so deployments are one click, monitoring and alerting, cost review to
stop paying for idle resources, backups and restore drills.

Good fit when: deployments are manual and scary, or the cloud bill is a mystery.

## 6. Maintenance and support

Monthly plans covering security updates, dependency upgrades, bug fixes, small
feature changes, uptime monitoring, backup verification, and a defined response
time for issues. Available for software we built and, after a code review, for
software someone else built.

## What we do not do

We would rather say this up front than waste a call:

- We do not do standalone graphic design or brand identity work. We do product
  and interface design for things we are building.
- We do not do paid ads, social media management or SEO content writing.
- We do not sell licences for other companies' software.
- We do not take on projects where the timeline is impossible. We will say so and
  propose what is achievable instead.
