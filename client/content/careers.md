## How to apply

Email **careers@praxontech.in** with:

1. Your resume (PDF).
2. One short paragraph on something you actually built, and what was hard about it.
3. Links — GitHub, a live project, anything we can open.

We read every email. We reply to applications we want to take forward, usually
within a week. Please do not send follow-up messages through the website chat —
the assistant cannot forward them and cannot check your application status.

## Open roles

**1. Full Stack Developer (1 to 3 years) — Hyderabad, hybrid**
React on the front end, Python or Node on the back end, PostgreSQL. You will work
directly on client projects from week one. We need someone who can read an
existing codebase without panicking.

**2. AI / ML Engineer (0 to 2 years) — Hyderabad, hybrid**
LLM integrations, RAG pipelines, embeddings, prompt design, evaluation. Strong
Python required. You do not need research experience; you do need to have built
something end to end and be able to explain why it worked.

**3. Frontend Developer (0 to 2 years) — Hyderabad, hybrid**
React, responsive CSS, real attention to how things look and feel on a phone.
Show us something you built that you are proud of.

**4. Internships (3 to 6 months) — Hyderabad**
Open to final-year and recently graduated engineering students in web
development, mobile development and AI/ML. Paid stipend. Interns work on real
project modules with a mentor, not on dummy tasks. A good internship can convert
to a full-time offer.

## What we look for

- You have built things, not only completed courses.
- You can explain your own code and say honestly when you do not know something.
- You write clearly in English, because clients read what you write.
- You finish what you start.

## What we offer

- Real client projects from the first month, not shadowing.
- Direct mentoring from senior engineers — the team is small enough that this
  actually happens.
- Hybrid working from our Hyderabad base.
- Compensation discussed during the interview process, based on the role and what
  you can demonstrably do.

## Hiring process

1. Email application.
2. A short technical conversation (30 to 45 minutes).
3. A small practical task, time-boxed and relevant to the role. Paid if it takes
   more than a few hours.
4. A final discussion covering the offer.

We do not run multi-round marathon interviews or unpaid month-long "trial
projects".

## Freshers

Yes, we hire freshers, both as interns and into junior roles. A degree matters
less to us than a repository we can open and read. If you are a fresher, the
paragraph about something you built is the most important part of your email.

Contact for all hiring matters: **careers@praxontech.in**
